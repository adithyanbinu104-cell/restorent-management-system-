## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a luxurious, nocturnal culinary environment reminiscent of private members' lounges, boutique chocolateries, and upscale espresso bars. It bridges tactile skeuomorphic luxury with structured, modern e-commerce utility. 

The aesthetic centers on rich espresso-stained wood textures, luminous amber backlighting, metallic gold trim, and precise typography. The experience is intimate, indulgent, and confident. Users are treated to an elevated dining narrative where high-resolution food assets pop dramatically off moody, dark-roasted surfaces.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from roasted cacao, dark espresso crema, and brushed antique brass. 

- **Primary (`#D4AF37`)**: Antique Gold. Used for prominent CTA buttons, badge borders, highlight states, key icons, and luxury accents.
- **Secondary (`#5A3825`)**: Roasted Mocha. Used for warm surface elevations, container fills, and secondary controls.
- **Tertiary (`#E6C687`)**: Champagne Gilt. Used for soft ambient glows, high-contrast labels, and warm metallic highlights.
- **Neutral (`#120D0A`)**: Espresso Noir. The core canvas background, providing deep contrast against ambient lighting and bright photography.

### Functional & Surface Shades
- **Surface Deep**: `#0C0806` (Bottom sheets, navigation bar backings, base foundation).
- **Surface Elevated**: `#1E1510` (Product cards, modal panels, elevated order cards).
- **Border Trim**: `#3D2E1E` (Subtle boundary borders) and `#D4AF37` at 25% to 60% opacity for illuminated metallic outlines.
- **Text Primary**: `#FDF8EE` (Warm ivory for absolute legibility on dark wood tones).
- **Text Secondary**: `#A89684` (Muted caramel for descriptive copy, metadata, and subtitles).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs classical hospitality warmth with clean, modern digital clarity:

- **Headlines (Domine):** A sturdy, authoritative serif with warm curves that echoes luxury menus, artisanal branding, and classic European bistros. Rendered in upper case or title case with slight positive tracking for titles and brand anchors.
- **Body (Manrope):** A geometric, modern sans-serif optimized for dark-mode screens. Ensures exceptional clarity for food descriptions, dietary notes, and basket summaries.
- **Labels & Numbers (Space Grotesk):** Engineered with crisp, slightly wide tabular proportions for currency, pricing, tags (e.g., `COLD`, `HOT`), and micro-interactions.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established not through harsh drop shadows, but through warm radial light leaks, metallic edge reflections, and tonal surfaces:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Deep coffee brown `#120D0A` overlaid with subtle vertical pinstripe grooving or fine texture.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Stalls):** `#1E1510` surfaces framed with a 1px border gradient (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)` on top blending to `rgba(61, 46, 30, 0.2)` on bottom).
- **Level 2 (Popovers & Modals):** `#251B14` surface with a diffuse ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75), 0 0 20px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Cart & Primary Action):** Rich brass linear gradient surfaces with an outward warm candle-like aura: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces tailored curvature—neither overly aggressive nor clinical:

- Standard cards, interactive tiles, and modal dialogs adopt a balanced `0.5rem` (8px) to `1rem` (16px) corner radius.
- Action pills (such as the quick-add `+` button, quantity stepper toggles, and status badges like `COLD` or `HOT`) utilize full pill silhouettes for organic thumb ergonomics.
- Key product photography hero frames employ classical architectural arches (curved upper perimeter with a squared base) to echo boutique cafe display windows.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Add to Basket", "To The Counter"):** Full pill or 12px rounded container with a rich golden metallic gradient (`linear-gradient(180deg, #E6C687 0%, #D4AF37 50%, #9B7720 100%)`). Black or deep espresso bold text (`#120D0A`) with subtle top inner highlight.
- **Secondary Action:** Translucent mocha fill (`rgba(90, 56, 37, 0.4)`) bordered by a 1px antique gold outline (`#D4AF37`).
- **Icon Quick Add (+):** Circular gold-bordered or solid antique gold disc with an espresso icon.

### Cards
- **Product Tiles:** Deep roasted background (`#1E1510`) with thin gold trim. Products break out of the top card boundary via floating imagery with gentle under-drop shadows. Bottom metadata section contains title, serving detail, price in gold monospace numerals, and the round add button.
- **Banner / Stall Header:** Expansive dark gradient container framed with traditional geometric filigree or micro-patterns along top and bottom edges.

### Quantity Steppers & Inputs
- **Steppers:** Enclosed capsule in muted roasted brown (`#2A1D15`) with gold minus/plus symbols and bold white center counters.
- **Search & Input Fields:** Inset coffee-black field with a subtle inner shadow, warm off-white placeholder text, and an illuminated gold border upon active focus.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills (Cold / Hot / Chef Special):** Compact rounded pills with light champagne fill (`#E6C687`) and dark brown micro-typography, or semi-transparent brown fill with a crisp gold stroke.
- **Currency Badges:** Gold coin emblem paired with bold numeral figures.
